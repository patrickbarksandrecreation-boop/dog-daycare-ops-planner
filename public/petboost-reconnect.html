PETBOOST CONNECTION

Renew your login.

Sign in to Petboost for the sync worker, from this phone.

Use the private reconnect link or enter your one-use code.

Request a fresh code from your administrator. Codes expire and work once.

Your login is sent securely to the planner’s worker browser. Passwords and verification codes aren’t saved on this phone. Only the encrypted Petboost session is retained. Reconnecting checks the login without importing bookings or changing groups.

Email/password and email verification codes are supported. Google, Apple, passkeys and CAPTCHA challenges require the desktop reconnect helper.
